## Add a download to Tonight

<Steps>

1. Press <kbd>⌘N</kbd> to open the Add sheet, or paste a link into the bar at the top of Ladle’s window and click **More options**.

2. Under **Start**, choose **Tonight, 1:00 AM**.

3. Add the download. It shows a **Tonight** tag and “Starts at 1:00 AM” in your list.

</Steps>

Click **Tonight** under **Queues** in Ladle’s sidebar to see everything that’s waiting. Changed your mind? Select a download and click **Start now** to start it straight away.

## What happens overnight

- At **1:00 AM**, Ladle starts the queue.
- At **7:00 AM**, it stops. Anything unfinished keeps its progress and carries on the next night.
- When the queue is done, Ladle **puts your Mac to sleep**, unless you’ve told it not to.

## Keep your Mac awake for 1:00 AM

Ladle can’t wake a sleeping Mac. For the queue to start on time, your Mac needs to be awake, with Ladle open, at 1:00 AM:

- **On a MacBook**, leave it plugged in with the lid open. Then open **System Settings › Battery**, click **Options…**, and turn on **Prevent automatic sleeping on power adapter when the display is off**.
- **On an iMac, Mac mini, Mac Studio or Mac Pro**, open **System Settings › Energy** (called **Energy Saver** in macOS Sonoma) and turn on **Prevent automatic sleeping when the display is off**.

The screen can still turn off; only the Mac itself needs to stay awake. Ladle opens at login out of the box, so it’s ready even after a restart.

<Aside type="caution">
**Plugged in?** With **Pause when running on battery** on, which it is out of the box, a MacBook on battery won’t run the queue. The same goes for **Pause on Low Data networks** if your Wi-Fi uses Low Data Mode. Both are in **Ladle › Settings › Schedule**.
</Aside>

## Change the schedule

Open **Ladle › Settings › Schedule**:

| Setting | Out of the box | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Tonight queue | 1:00 AM to 7:00 AM | When the queue starts and stops. |
| Run on | Every day | The nights the queue runs. Click a day to turn it off or on. |
| When the queue finishes | Put the Mac to sleep | Choose **Do nothing** to leave your Mac awake. |
| Pause when running on battery | On | Holds the queue while your Mac runs on battery. |
| Pause on Low Data networks | On | Holds the queue on networks with macOS Low Data Mode on. |
