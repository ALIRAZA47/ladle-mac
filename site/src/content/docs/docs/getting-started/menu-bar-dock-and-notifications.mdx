## The menu bar

Ladle’s icon in the menu bar shows your total download speed. Click it for a menu with:

- **Link on your clipboard**, when you’ve copied a link to a file, with a button to download it.
- What’s **downloading** right now, with each file’s progress.
- What’s **up next**, including anything waiting for the Tonight queue.
- A **Limit speed** switch that caps your total speed, handy when you’re on a video call.
- **Open Ladle**, to bring up the main window.

Don’t want it there? Turn off **Show Ladle in the menu bar** in **Ladle › Settings › General**.

## The Dock

While files are downloading, Ladle’s Dock icon shows a progress bar for everything at once and a badge with how many files are downloading.

You can also drop links, or `.webloc` files from Finder, onto Ladle’s Dock icon to add them.

## Notifications

When a download finishes, Ladle sends a notification with **Open** and **Show in Finder** buttons.

These settings live in **Ladle › Settings › General**:

<SettingsTable>

| Setting | Default | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Notify me when downloads finish | On | Sends a notification for each finished download. |
| Play a sound too | Off | Adds a sound to that notification. |

</SettingsTable>

The first time Ladle wants to notify you, macOS asks whether to allow it. To change your answer later, open **System Settings › Notifications** and click **Ladle**.

Prefer to have the file open, or shown in Finder, as soon as it’s done? Change **When a download finishes** in **Ladle › Settings › Downloads**.
