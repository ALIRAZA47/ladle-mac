title: Safer hand-offs
---

A security update from a review of the whole app before its public launch. Ladle installs it on its own.

- **The video button keeps your accounts to itself.** Ladle now looks at a video’s stream without your cookies unless the stream comes from the page’s own site, so a web page can’t learn anything about you by showing you someone else’s video. The button only answers your own mouse and clicks, never a page’s script.
- **Links from web pages ask properly.** A `ladle://` link opened by a web page can no longer add cookies or a referrer to the download, claim a size, or pretend to come from your browser. The panel now says “A web page asked Ladle to download”, shows the file’s real address, and Ladle contacts no server until you choose Download.
- **Cookies stay on https.** Cookies your browser passed along are no longer sent again when a site redirects from https to plain http or to another port, and other sites get only the page’s address, not its full URL, like a browser does.
- **Names stay in the folder.** A download can never be named `.` or `..`, and a download never replaces a folder, even with “If a file with the same name exists” set to Replace.

If you added the Chrome extension in developer mode, download the new `Ladle-Chrome-Extension.zip` and load it again: that copy doesn’t update itself.
