title: The first pour
---

The first release of Ladle, a free download manager for the Mac.

- **Fast downloads.** Each file is split into 8 connections out of the box, up to 32. When a connection finishes early, it takes over half of the biggest piece left.
- **Resumes and retries.** Downloads carry on after you quit, after sleep and after a network change, on servers that support it. Failed pieces retry on their own, and Ladle backs off when a server asks it to.
- **Every way in.** Paste into the link bar (⌘K), add lists and numbered series like `ep-[01-12].mp3` in the Add sheet (⌘N), copy a link and get a nudge, drag links onto the window (or `.webloc` files onto the Dock), or use `ladle://` links.
- **Browser extension for Chrome, Arc, Edge and Brave.** Catches downloads as you click them, adds Download with Ladle and Download All Links with Ladle… to the context menu, and puts a Download video button on videos you’re allowed to save. Until it’s in the Chrome Web Store, you add it in developer mode; [Set up your browser](https://ladle.alirazakhan.me/docs/getting-started/set-up-your-browser/#chrome) shows how. The Firefox add-on is on its way to Firefox Add-ons. Safari’s extension can’t run in this release, because Safari only loads extensions from apps signed by a registered Apple developer.
- **Queues.** The main queue runs 3 downloads at a time. The Tonight queue runs from 1:00 to 7:00 AM and can put your Mac to sleep when it’s done.
- **Tidy folders.** Files land in `~/Downloads/Ladle`, sorted into Video, Music, Documents, Archives, Apps and Images, with SHA-256 and MD5 checksum checks.
- **Around your Mac.** A menu bar menu, progress on the Dock icon, and notifications when downloads finish.
- **Updates itself** in the background, once a day.

**The first time you open Ladle**, macOS asks you to approve it, because Ladle is free and isn’t notarized by Apple. [First launch](https://ladle.alirazakhan.me/docs/getting-started/install/#first-launch) shows how. It only asks once: updates are checked against Ladle’s own signing key and install without asking.
